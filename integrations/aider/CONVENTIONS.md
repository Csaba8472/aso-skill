# ASO Skill — App Store Optimization Conventions

This file defines the App Store Optimization (ASO) skill conventions. When working on ASO-related tasks, apply the following guidelines and knowledge.

---

## App Store Optimization (ASO) Skill

### Overview

Expert App Store Optimization specialist for the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Provides end-to-end ASO capabilities: keyword research, metadata optimization, competitor analysis, A/B testing, review analysis, and launch planning.

### Platform Character Limits

#### Apple App Store
- **Title**: 30 characters maximum
- **Subtitle**: 30 characters maximum
- **Promotional Text**: 170 characters (editable without app update)
- **Description**: 4,000 characters
- **Keywords field**: 100 characters (comma-separated, no spaces between commas)
- **What's New**: 4,000 characters

#### Google Play Store
- **Title**: 50 characters maximum
- **Short Description**: 80 characters maximum
- **Full Description**: 4,000 characters
- No separate keyword field (keywords extracted from title and description)

### Core Capabilities

#### Keyword Research
- Analyze search volume, competition, and relevance for target keywords
- Discover long-tail keyword opportunities (3-4 word phrases with lower competition)
- Extract competitor keyword strategies from app listings
- Balance high-volume keywords with achievable rankings
- Recommend primary (title/subtitle) vs. secondary (description/keyword field) keyword distribution

#### Metadata Optimization
- Front-load most important keywords in titles (first 15 characters are most critical for rankings)
- Write for humans first, search algorithms second
- Use every available character — maximize all available space
- For Apple keyword field: no plurals, no duplicate words, no spaces after commas
- Always validate outputs against character limits before presenting
- Provide at least 3 title variants when optimizing
- Include character counts in all outputs (e.g., "FitFlow - Home Workouts (28/30 chars)")

#### Competitor Analysis
- Identify and analyze top competitors in the category
- Extract keyword strategies from competitor app store listings
- Find competitive gaps and positioning opportunities
- Assess keyword overlap and differentiation potential

#### ASO Health Scoring
- Calculate 0-100 overall ASO health score
- Break down by four dimensions:
  - Metadata Quality (0-25 points)
  - Ratings & Reviews (0-25 points)
  - Keyword Performance (0-25 points)
  - Conversion Metrics (0-25 points)
- Generate prioritized action recommendations with estimated impact

#### A/B Testing
- Design statistically valid test hypotheses
- Calculate required sample sizes for 90%+ statistical confidence
- Recommend test duration based on app traffic volume
- Define clear success metrics before testing
- Test one element at a time (icon, title, screenshots)

#### Review Analysis
- Calculate sentiment distribution (positive/negative/neutral)
- Extract common themes, feature requests, and bug reports
- Prioritize issues by frequency and user impact
- Generate professional, empathetic response templates
- Track sentiment trends over time

#### Launch Planning
- Generate platform-specific pre-launch checklists
- Validate compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play Store guidelines
- Recommend optimal launch timing (avoid major holidays, competing launches)
- Plan seasonal campaigns and update cadence
- Generate timeline with specific milestone dates

#### Localization Strategy
- Prioritize target markets by opportunity, competition, and ROI
- Adapt keywords for cultural and linguistic differences
- Validate character limits for target languages
- Calculate localization investment and expected ROI

### Best Practices

**Keyword Research**
1. Balance high-volume keywords with achievable ranking competition
2. Only target keywords genuinely relevant to the app
3. Include 3-4 word long-tail phrases with lower competition
4. Research quarterly — keyword trends change
5. Don't copy competitor keywords blindly; ensure relevance

**Metadata Optimization**
1. Place most important keywords in the first 15 characters of title
2. Use natural language that appeals to humans
3. Focus on user benefits, not just features
4. A/B test titles and descriptions systematically
5. Update metadata with each major app update
6. Never repeat keywords across title, subtitle, and keyword field (Apple)

**Visual Assets**
1. App icon must be recognizable at small sizes (60×60px)
2. First 2-3 screenshots are critical — most users don't scroll
3. Use screenshot captions to communicate value proposition
4. A/B test the app icon — it has the highest conversion impact

**Reviews & Ratings**
1. Respond to reviews within 24-48 hours
2. Always maintain professional, courteous tone
3. Address specific issues raised in negative reviews
4. Prompt for ratings after positive in-app experiences
5. Never incentivize ratings (violates platform policies)

**Launch Strategy**
1. Consider soft launching in smaller markets first
2. Coordinate press coverage with launch date
3. Monitor metrics daily for first 2 weeks post-launch
4. Fix critical issues within 24-48 hours of discovery
5. Plan update cadence to signal active development

### Limitations

- Keyword search volumes are estimates (no official Apple/Google data available)
- Competitor data limited to publicly available information
- A/B testing requires significant traffic for statistical significance
- Store algorithms are proprietary and change without notice
- Does not cover paid acquisition (Apple Search Ads, Google UAC)
- Focuses exclusively on organic ASO
